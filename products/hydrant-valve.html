Hydrant Valves

Redway hydrant valves are manufactured from high-grade gunmetal and brass castings to withstand working pressures up to 21 kg/cm². Available in single-headed and double-headed landing valve configurations, they comply with IS 5290 and BS 5041 standards. The precision-machined seat and spindle assembly ensures drip-free shut-off even after extended dormant periods. Every valve is hydrostatically tested before dispatch to guarantee leak-proof performance on site.
